# Credits

QFX original statusbar textures were created for QFXUnitframes.

Additional statusbar textures were derived from CC0 source images on OpenGameArt and converted to grayscale 256x32 TGA textures for LibSharedMedia statusbar use:

- QFX Complex Gradient 1, QFX Complex Gradient 2, QFX Complex Gradient 5: derived from "CC0 Complex Gradient Textures" by PuzzleAndy.
- QFX 3D Glass: derived from "health bars" by interglactic.
- QFX Gloss Fill: derived from "Free Simple Health Bar Assets" by maxhacker11.
- QFX Dark Bevel: derived from "Health bar kit." by Prime_8.

Source pages:

- https://opengameart.org/content/cc0-complex-gradient-textures
- https://opengameart.org/content/health-bars
- https://opengameart.org/content/free-simple-health-bar-assets
- https://opengameart.org/content/health-bar-kit

Local live extracts:

- QFX Live Player Health / mMediaTag G1: copied from `!mMT_MediaPack` as `g1.tga`.
- QFX Live Nameplate / mMediaTag B4: copied from `!mMT_MediaPack` as `b4.tga`.
- QFX Live Nameplate Slant 60: local derivative of `mMediaTag B4` with approximately 60 degree angled separators.
- QFX Live Party Frames / mMediaTag B1: copied from `!mMT_MediaPack` as `b1.tga`.

These local extracts and derivatives are for private/local use only. The source `!mMT_MediaPack` license says its textures may not be redistributed.
